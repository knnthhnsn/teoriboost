# Codex image brief: three road graphics for TeoriBoost

Use your image generation tool (image_gen.imagegen). Attached: `docs/redesign/concepts/ref-racecar.png`, one of
the app's own cars, for the illustration style (flat colours, soft single shading, clean shapes, a friendly cartoon
look). Our brand colours: signal yellow `#FFD60A`, asphalt `#1F2328`, white. Flat illustration, no photo look, no
3D render, no text, no logos, no brand names, no background elements beyond what is asked.

1. `docs/redesign/concepts/car-top.png` (1024 x 1536, portrait): a small, friendly yellow (`#FFD60A`) hatchback seen
   from DIRECTLY ABOVE, driving straight up the image, centred, about 40 % of the image height. Dark tinted
   windscreen and rear window, a black roof stripe pair down the middle, small side mirrors, the four tyres just
   visible at the corners. Soft single shading, one simple white shine on the bonnet. Perfectly flat plain
   background `#FF00FF` (magenta, so it can be cut out), nothing else.

2. `docs/redesign/concepts/tyre.png` (1536 x 1536): ONE big car tyre seen straight from the side, filling about
   90 % of the image: a chunky black rubber tyre with a visible tread pattern around its edge and a little sidewall
   lettering texture (abstract marks, not readable text), and a dark graphite five-spoke alloy rim with a yellow
   (`#FFD60A`) centre cap. Flat illustration with soft shading and one white shine arc on the tyre. Perfectly flat
   plain background `#FF00FF` (magenta), nothing else.

3. `docs/redesign/concepts/skid.png` (2048 x 1024, landscape): two parallel tyre skid marks (the pair left by a
   car's two wheels) sweeping across the whole image in a long, smooth curve from bottom left to upper right,
   leaving the frame on both sides. Each mark is a band of chunky, slightly broken tread blocks, like rubber
   pressed on asphalt, and it fades and breaks up a little towards the ends. PURE WHITE marks on a PURE BLACK
   (`#000000`) background, nothing else, no grey asphalt texture (it will be turned into a transparent overlay).

Do not modify any other files.
