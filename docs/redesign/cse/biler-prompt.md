# TeoriBoost — semi-realistisk 2D-cel-animation, v5

Værktøj: indbygget image_gen.imagegen. Input 1: det oprindelige bilark, alene for identiteter, proportioner og opdeling. Input 2: brugerens varevogn som formreference. Brugerens stilbeskrivelse styrer farveflader, konturer og skygger.

```text
Use case: style-transfer.
Restyle the eight-vehicle sheet as SEMI-REALISTIC 2D CEL ANIMATION.

Image 1 is the edit target and the reference for the vehicle identities, realistic proportions, perspective, colors and four-column / two-row layout.
Image 2, the user's gray van, is a reference for believable everyday vehicle construction and quiet, subdued visual character. The written rendering requirements below take priority over that reference's soft painted lighting.

RENDERING REQUIREMENTS:
- Real-world proportions and normal-size tires. No exaggerated cabins, no cute/cartoon squashing, no eyes or faces.
- Fine, uniform dark-gray linework, like restrained ligne claire. Use the SAME delicate hairline weight on the outer silhouette and on door handles, panel seams, trim, window frames and wheel details. At a roughly 2000-pixel-wide sheet, lines should look approximately one pixel thin, never bold, heavy, brushy or doubled. No special thick silhouette border.
- Flat opaque color fills. Each material has its base color and ONE darker, sharply bounded shadow color. No gradient anywhere on the vehicles. No soft blended light, no glossy 3D shine, no rendered metallic highlights. A few hard-edged pale reflection shapes on glass are allowed.
- Muted dusty gray-blue, beige, olive and faded pastel colors, with modest amber lamps. Restored paint remains subdued.
- Everyday wear is selective and graphic: a few restrained flat rust patches, scuffs or a slight dent, drawn with the same thin line. No stippled noise, painterly mottling, heavy grime or realistic surface texture.
- Accurate yet quiet interiors visible through the glass: simple seats, steering wheel and steering column, in the same flat-color language.

Preserve the eight recognizable vehicles and their matched pairs: worn cream scooter above restored ochre scooter; worn small hatchback above restored petrol-blue hatchback; worn long Scandinavian estate above restored dusty-olive estate; worn ivory race coupe above restored ivory coupe with ochre stripe. The upper row is worn, the lower row restored. Same right-facing front three-quarter side view, same geometry in each pair.

This is a complete change of RENDERING from the source sheet: redraw the shading as flat cel areas and the edges as fine even lines. Do not preserve its painted texture, diffuse shading or 3D modeling appearance.
Make the sheet clean and spacious, with all eight full silhouettes separated and every mirror and tire inside the canvas. Genuine transparent alpha background. No ground shadows, background scene, text, logos, labels, halos or checkerboard.
```
