# Official context for Køreskoler pages

Checked 2026-11-10 (Europe/Copenhagen). The local `data/raw/official-*` files are research snapshots. Their presence does **not** confer a republication licence for page text, design, images, or map code. Use the short factual points below in original wording and link to the authority.

## Licence process and tests (category B)

- The [Færdselsstyrelsen first-licence guide](https://www.fstyr.dk/privat/koerekort/dit-foerste-koerekort/tag-koerekort-saadan-goer-du) describes an approved application and first-aid certificate before theory booking. The student can book on MinKøreprøve or appoint a proxy, such as the instructor. The practical test can be booked after the theory test is passed and the practical modules are completed. A school car must be arranged for the practical test; a private car cannot be used. The practical test must be passed within 18 months of the theory test. These details depend on the student's case; town pages should link to the guide rather than present a universal checklist.
- [Færdselsstyrelsen's test guide](https://www.fstyr.dk/privat/koerekort/proever/koereproeve) distinguishes the theoretical and practical parts. Its practical-test description gives roughly 30–45 minutes. Do not imply a test-centre address is a theory-test centre merely because it appears in the car-test map.
- For students who started a new driving education from 2026-10-01, [Færdselsstyrelsen says](https://www.fstyr.dk/erhverv/koereskoler-og-koerelaerere/frigivelse-og-afholdelse-af-proever) tests are booked on minkøreprøve.dk. Existing students finish their booking process in Køreprøvebooking. Avoid a single unconditional booking instruction.
- The first-licence guide says digital theory is offered at **16 sites**. Its [test-place map](https://www.fstyr.dk/privat/koerekort/proever) has separate tabs for theory, practical car, motorcycle, truck/bus, and tractor/motor equipment. The business-facing page says car tests are held in **57 towns**. These are different counts, and neither is a count of all driving schools.

## Practical car-test place data

`data/raw/official-fstyr-practical-car-sites.json` is a factual extraction from the **“Praktisk prøve: Bil”** tab of the [official map](https://www.fstyr.dk/privat/koerekort/proever), fetched 2026-11-10. It has **61 marker records and 60 distinct address strings**. Two separate markers point to Odinsvej 23, 4100 Ringsted (Applus and Færdselsstyrelsen Ringsted); preserve that ambiguity if using the data. The theory tab had 39 markers in the fetched HTML. The same five-tab map was present on the [køreprøve page](https://www.fstyr.dk/privat/koerekort/proever/koereproeve).

For a school page, “nearest practical test site” can only mean nearest by straight-line distance from the school's verified coordinates to a category-B marker in this dated snapshot. Label it as an approximate geographic comparison. It does not establish the actual booking site, exam availability, catchment, route, travel time, or affiliation with a school. The student and instructor choose the actual practical-test place, subject to what booking offers; the authority advises agreeing on it with the instructor. Recheck the official map before release and periodically after release.

## Driving-school registry feasibility

- [Statistics Denmark's current DB25 classification](https://www.dst.dk/da/Statistik/dokumentation/nomenklaturer/db) lists **85.53.00 (855300), “Drift af køreskoler.”** The older DB07 label was “Køreskoler”; the numeric code remains 855300. Industry membership alone does not verify a particular school location, licence categories, current teaching activity, public phone number, or website.
- [Erhvervsstyrelsen](https://erhvervsstyrelsen.dk/det-centrale-virksomhedsregister-cvr) says CVR basic company data are public and include name, business address, branch, status dates, and an advertising-protection flag. Its caveat matters if records are used for direct marketing or republished with contact information. A registry address may be a one-person school's home/business address; apply the brief's personal-data boundary before a public school page.
- The current [Datafordeler CVR GraphQL product](https://datafordeler.dk/dataoversigt/det-centrale-virksomhedsregister-cvr/cvr-graphql/) exposes non-CVRPerson entities without a *special CVR access request*, but still requires creating a Datafordeler user, IT system, and API key. CVRPerson is restricted. The legacy REST route also has access and IP-listing requirements and is being phased out in late 2026. Thus a **credential-free bulk CVR pull by 855300 is not established** by the official service. Do not claim one or collect an account/key in this project.
- [Datafordeler's CVR terms](https://datafordeler.dk/vejledning/brugervilkaar/det-centrale-virksomhedsregister-cvr/) apply **CC BY 4.0** to free CVR basic data and require credit to “Det Centrale Virksomhedsregister (CVR).” This is a licence for CVR data, not for third-party directories or Færdselsstyrelsen's website text.

## Address search after DAWA

- [Klimadatastyrelsen announced](https://www.klimadatastyrelsen.dk/om-klimadatastyrelsen/nyheder/nyhedsarkiv/2026/jul/dawa-lukker-d-1-oktober-2026) complete DAWA shutdown on **2026-10-01 at 10:00**. The brief's suggested `api.dataforsyningen.dk` DAWA address endpoint is therefore obsolete for autocomplete.
- The official replacement is [Adressevælger](https://confluence.kds.dk/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=234782998). Its [fonetisk søgning documentation](https://confluence.kds.dk/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=244318431) gives `https://adressevaelger.dk/husnumre/soeg?tekst=...&token=...` for street-number suggestions, and `/adresser/soeg` for full unit addresses. The API returns a DAR ID and display label for a specific result. A follow-up `/husnumre/{id}?token=...` returns more fields and map coordinates in `EPSG:25832`; **do not interpret its X/Y coordinates as WGS84 longitude/latitude**. Convert before map or distance calculations.
- [KDS access guidance](https://confluence.kds.dk/display/ADV/Brugerstyring) says user accounts are not yet required but a token query parameter is mandatory; it publishes a shared example value. The project brief disallows tokens and keys, so **do not integrate Adressevælger into this build**. It expects account controls in late 2026 or early 2027. KDS's [FAQ](https://confluence.kds.dk/display/ADV/FAQ) says output and behavior are not 1:1 with DAWA, and reverse geocoding is absent. For this build, local suggestions from the existing OSM-derived town/postcode data can be combined with a user-triggered address lookup under a separately checked no-key provider policy, or a town-only search.

## Reuse boundaries and source snapshots

I did not find an explicit licence for republishing the agency's prose, images, or full map dataset. Use independently worded facts and source links; do not copy its descriptions or graphics. The extracted test-place JSON is an internal source trace, not a claim that the authority endorses the directory.
