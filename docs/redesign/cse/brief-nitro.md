# Codex image brief: nitro exhaust flames and wind streaks for the TeoriBoost race

Use your image generation tool (image_gen.imagegen). Attached: `docs/redesign/cse/streak-flammer.png` (our streak
flames: the exact flame style to follow: fine dark ink outline, flat cel colours, one sharply edged shadow tone, chunky
rounded tongues) and `docs/redesign/cse/biler.png` (our vehicles, for the overall Common Side Effects look).

The vehicles drive to the RIGHT. Draw the fire that blasts out BEHIND them (out of the exhaust), so every flame points
LEFT: its narrow root sits at the RIGHT edge of the image, vertically centred, and it widens and breaks into tongues
towards the left. Same Common Side Effects style as the streak flames: fine ink outlines, flat colours, no gradients,
no glow, no blur, no smoke, no sparks flying loose, no faces, no text.

1. `nitro-1.png`, `nitro-2.png`, `nitro-3.png`: a lively nitro flame. Colours: a pale cyan-white core at the root
   (#E8FBFF), turning to signal yellow (#FFD60A) and orange (#FF8A00) with red-orange tips (#E8431B). Three frames of
   the SAME flame flickering (the tongues shift a little between frames, the root stays exactly in place, same size).
2. `mega-1.png`, `mega-2.png`, `mega-3.png`: the same flame, but huge and violent: about twice as long and 1.5 x as
   tall, a longer cyan-white nitro core with a hint of electric blue (#38C8FF) along it, more tongues, a few detached
   flame droplets just behind it. Three flicker frames, root exactly in place, same size.
3. `wind-1.png`: 5 to 7 horizontal speed / wind streaks for a vehicle going very fast, drawn in the same ink style
   (each a long thin rounded stroke, white fill #FFFFFF with a fine dark ink outline, different lengths, loosely
   stacked), nothing else.

Every image a landscape 1536 x 512 PNG on a perfectly flat plain background #FF00FF (magenta, for cutting out). The
flames' root touches the right edge at the vertical centre; the nitro flame fills about 55 % of the width, the mega
flame about 95 %. Save into `docs/redesign/cse/nitro/`. Do not modify any other files.
