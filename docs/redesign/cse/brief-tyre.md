# Codex image brief: the Min bil tyre in the TeoriBoost (Common Side Effects) style

Use your image generation tool (image_gen.imagegen). Attached:
- `docs/redesign/concepts/tyre.png`: the tyre we use now. Keep its composition (one big car tyre seen almost from
  the side, turned a little towards us, a five-spoke alloy rim with five wheel nuts and a yellow centre cap, a chunky
  off-road tread on the edge), but NOT its look: it is too slick, vector-like and glossy.
- `docs/redesign/cse/biler.png` and `docs/redesign/cse/sneglen-og-keglen.png`: our style. Follow it exactly: fine,
  slightly uneven dark ink outlines, flat muted cel colours with one sharply edged shadow tone, a little hand-drawn
  texture and wear (scuffs on the rubber, a bit of dust and grime in the rim), no gradients, no glow, no lens
  highlights, no plastic shine.

Colours: rubber in warm charcoal (#2B2F33, shadow #1F2328, worn edges slightly lighter), the rim in a muted gunmetal
grey with a pale grey edge, the centre cap signal yellow (#FFD60A, shadow #C9A200). No face, no eyes, no text, no
logo, no people, nothing else in the picture.

One square 1024 x 1024 PNG, the tyre filling about 90 % of the image, centred, on a perfectly flat plain background
#FF00FF (magenta, for cutting out). Save the image tool's output as it is to `docs/redesign/cse/tyre.png` (no local
post-processing is needed, and do not ask any questions: finish on your own). Do not modify any other files.
