# Bounded firsthand flow observations

Inspected in the actual browser on 2026-10-16. These are two accessible examples, not a ranking or proof of best-in-market status. No competitor text, question bank, images or video was imported into Natkørsel.

## Teoriklar, Denmark

Entry: https://www.teoriklar.dk/559/online-teoriproever

Observed path: choose digital tests → choose free quick test → instructions in modal → start → first question → question overview. The first question has a prominent rendered driver view with mirrors/dashboard, adjacent multi-select answers, read-aloud and save-for-later controls. The overview distinguishes unanswered, answered and marked questions and allows direct navigation. Only the beginning and overview were exercised; no pass or paid-course review is claimed.

Useful design decision: ground observation tasks in the actual driver's available view, including rearward information. Keep exam selection neutral and allow revisiting. Do not transfer its marketing claims into our evidence.

## Safe Driving for Life, UK / DVSA publishing partner

Entry: https://www.safedrivingforlife.info/free-practice-tests/hazard-perception-test/

Observed path: hazard-test instructions → start test → clip-specific instructions → play clip. The visible first video is forward-looking road footage; the interaction is to mark developing hazards as they emerge. The instructions describe registered-response flags and a timing-based score. The first clip playback was visually inspected, but its scoring/result flow and remaining clips were not completed.

Useful design decision: a developing traffic situation needs time, not merely a caption on a still. Our lesson separates identifying a relevant observation from choosing an action; it does not import the UK scoring system or rules into Danish theory.

## Natkørsel baseline and chosen intervention

Baseline `#journey:sving-cykel`: first screen is a prose card, no traffic scene, and a next-card action. Three authored cards precede the application scenario. The new exemplar starts in the situation, offers a right-side view, records a decision, animates its consequence and changes road geometry for the next exercise. Whether that improves human learning remains to be measured.
