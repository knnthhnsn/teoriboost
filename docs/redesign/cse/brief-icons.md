# Codex image brief: the TeoriBoost app icons in the Common Side Effects style

Use your image generation tool (image_gen.imagegen). Attached: `docs/redesign/cse/sneglen-og-keglen.png` and
`docs/redesign/cse/biler.png`, the STYLE: the animated series Common Side Effects. Fine, confident, slightly
irregular dark ink outlines, flat muted cel colours with one sharply edged shadow tone, tactile hand-drawn
imperfection. Palette: smoky teal, dusty olive, faded mustard (#E8B530), rust-orange, warm cream, charcoal, with a
signal yellow #FFD60A accent where it helps. No gradients, no glow, no 3D render, no text, no letters, no numbers.

These are APP ICONS: one simple object each, front-on or slight three-quarter view, chunky and readable at 28 px,
centred, filling about 80 % of the canvas, NO face, no character, no background shape or tile. Each a square
512 x 512 PNG with a real TRANSPARENT alpha background (no backdrop, no checkerboard, no shadow on the ground).
Keep all of them one consistent family (same line weight, shading and palette). Save as
`docs/redesign/cse/icons/<name>.png`:

- `book` (Lær): an open textbook, cream pages, teal cover
- `wheel` (Øv): a steering wheel, charcoal with a mustard centre
- `exam` (Prøve): a clipboard with a test sheet and a green tick
- `car` (Min bil): a small mustard car seen from the side
- `ai` (Spørg AI): a cream speech bubble with a small yellow spark in it
- `profile` (Din profil): a driving licence card with a simple head silhouette on it
- `target` (dagens mål): a dartboard target with a dart in the bullseye, rust-orange and cream rings
- `calendar`: a tear-off desk calendar
- `clock`: an alarm clock
- `trend`: a small rising bar chart with an arrow
- `gauge`: a speedometer dial with a needle
- `list`: a checklist note with three ticked lines
- `stop`: a red octagonal stop sign (blank, no letters)
- `bike`: a bicycle
- `yield`: a red-bordered upside-down triangle give-way sign (blank)
- `light`: a traffic light with the green lamp lit
- `clipboard`: a driving instructor's clipboard with a pen

Do not modify any other files.
