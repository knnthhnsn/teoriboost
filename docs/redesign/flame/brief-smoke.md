# Codex image brief: the streak flame's "out" state as smoke

Use your image generation tool (image_gen.imagegen). Attached:
1. `docs/redesign/flame/flame-happy.png` - our flame character (keep its face style, flatness, shine and size).
2. `docs/redesign/flame/reference.jpg` - the style reference sheet.

When the student's streak is lost, the flame has gone out and turned into a little puff of SMOKE. It must read
clearly as smoke, not as a grey blob or a ghost:

- Shape: a soft rounded smoke cloud, made of a few overlapping puffy circles, that rises into one or two thin
  curling wisps on top (like smoke curling up from a blown-out candle). The bottom is a small round puff,
  narrower than the flame was.
- Colour: flat light grey `#C9D1D9` with the inner overlaps a touch darker `#AEB8C2` so the puffs read as
  separate curls; the wisps fade lighter towards the tip. The same single white shine on the upper left as the
  flame. No outline, no drop shadow, no background elements.
- Face: the same eye style as the flame (white eyes, `#1F2328` pupils), sleepy half-closed lids and a tiny
  "o" mouth as if it just went "puff". Sad but cute, not gloomy.

Make it as ONE square 1024 x 1024 image, centred, about 60 % of the height, on a perfectly flat plain
background `#FFE0F0` (a pink that is far from grey, so it can be cut out), and save it as
`docs/redesign/flame/flame-smoke.png`. Do not modify any other files.
