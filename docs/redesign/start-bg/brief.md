# Codex image brief: tuner-garage background for the TeoriBoost start page

Use image_gen.imagegen. Make TWO images and save them exactly here (do not modify any other files):
1. `docs/redesign/start-bg/garage-wide.png` — landscape, about 1536×1024
2. `docs/redesign/start-bg/garage-tall.png` — portrait, about 1024×1536

Same scene in both: the inside of a friendly, tidy tuner workshop / garage, seen straight on.
- A back wall with a pegboard holding a few tools (spanners, a screwdriver, a tyre gauge) drawn as simple shapes,
  a half-open roll-up garage door on one side with a glimpse of a quiet road and sky outside,
  a small stack of tyres and a red rolling tool cabinet near the sides, a couple of hanging work lamps.
- A clean concrete floor. In the CENTER of the floor there must be a large EMPTY open area (a vehicle will be
  placed there by the app): no objects, no vehicle, nothing in the middle third of the image.
- Keep all props near the left and right edges and along the top of the wall, so the middle stays calm.

Art style (match the attached vehicle art): thick confident black outlines, completely flat colours, at most one
flat shadow tone, simple bold shapes. Palette: LIGHT and MUTED so a bright foreground vehicle stands out —
soft warm greys, pale blue-grey wall, cream, small accents of muted teal and muted lime green. Low contrast overall.
No people, no animals, no vehicles, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand names, no signs with writing.
