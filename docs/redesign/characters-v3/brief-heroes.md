# Codex image brief: Natkørsel mascots, hero style exploration

Use your image generation tool (image_gen.imagegen). Generate exactly 9 images: 3 characters × 3 styles.
Save each PNG to `docs/redesign/characters-v3/<slug>/hero-<style>.png` in this repository
(slugs: mira, knur, tordis; styles: 3d, flat, book). Do not modify any other files.
When done, list the saved file paths.

## Shared direction (all images)
- An original mascot for a Danish learning app for 17–30-year-olds. Premium craft, the appeal level of
  top app mascots, but completely original: must NOT resemble Duolingo, Tolan, Pokémon, Finch,
  Headspace, Moomins or any existing character.
- "Aged-up cute": charming with attitude and wit, not babyish.
- One character only, full body, centred, 3/4 view, expressive pose, strong readable silhouette.
- Plain flat cream background (#F6F3EE), soft contact shadow, no scenery, no text, no logos, no frame.
- Absolutely nothing related to cars, roads, traffic, signs, lamps or schools.

## Styles
- `3d`: stylized 3D character render, soft toy-like materials, gentle subsurface scattering,
  rounded appealing forms, soft studio lighting.
- `flat`: bold modern 2D vector illustration, chunky simple shapes, subtle soft shading, no outlines,
  sticker-ready.
- `book`: Scandinavian picture-book illustration, textured gouache / risograph print feel,
  slightly naive but confident shapes, limited palette.

## Characters

### mira: Mira Muld, the star-nosed mole astronomer
Confident, warm, nerdy, sure she is the best astronomer underground although she has never seen
the sky. Upright pear-shaped body, short dense velvet fur in soft violet (#7A6496) with a lighter
lavender sheen. Her signature is a bright pink (#E8608A) star-shaped nose made of ~22 short, chubby,
rounded petal-like rays (never thin tentacles). Tiny pinprick dark eyes (moles barely see), expressive
comma-shaped tufts as eyebrows, big pink paddle-shaped digging hands, small pink feet, short tail.
Pose: proudly holding up a tiny shiny pebble towards the viewer like a precious star she discovered,
chin up, eyes half-closed with pride. Not brown, no hat, no glasses, never shown as a head in a hole.

### knur: Knur, the deadpan red gurnard
A grumpy, deadpan fish from Danish waters whose face never admits anything while his huge wings give
away every feeling. Armoured wedge-shaped head in coral red (#E2553A) with heavy flat bony brow plates,
half-lidded unimpressed eyes, a wide slightly downturned mouth fighting a hidden smile. Peach belly
(#FFD2B3). Walks on three short finger-like fin rays per side. His signature: two huge fan-shaped pectoral
fins in turquoise (#1A9483) with radiating ribs and an electric-blue (#4F7CFF) scalloped edge, spread wide
like butterfly wings in excitement. Pose: face completely deadpan while the wings are flared open in
obvious joy. Never pink fins, no big round Pixar fish eyes.

### tordis: Tordis, the 66-million-year-old thunderstone
A fossil heart urchin turned to flint at Stevns Klint, Denmark. An immortal hypochondriac: survived
the dinosaur-killing meteor but panics over small things. Body: a low, wide, smooth dome (like a
loaf), polished dark sage-green flint (#5F7D72) with patches of chalk-white crust (#EFE6D2). On top, a
five-petal star pattern of small dotted pores engraved INTO the stone (like a real fossil sea urchin),
with the grooves glowing lime (#CFF24A). Small heart-shaped notch at the front bottom where her mouth
is. Big expressive worried eyes with chalky brow ridges, tiny stubby legs, two small stubby arms.
Pose: dramatic nervous gasp, little arms up, yet clearly sturdy as a rock. NO leaves, NO crown, NO
plant or cactus look, no pointed cone, not a rock with a face only.
