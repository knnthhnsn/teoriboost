# Codex image brief: the TeoriBoost streak flame (character)

Use your image generation tool (image_gen.imagegen). The attached image `docs/redesign/flame/reference.jpg`
is a sheet of nine flame characters by another artist. Use it as the STYLE reference: match its shape
language, flatness and simple faces closely. Do not copy any single character exactly; design our own
flame in that style.

## The character
A small, lovable flame mascot for a Danish driving-theory app. It shows how many days in a row the
student has practised. It must read at 24 px (a chip icon) and still look great at 200 px.

- Silhouette: one soft, blobby flame body, a bit wider at the bottom, with 3 rounded flame tips on top
  (the middle tip tallest, leaning slightly to one side) and a softly scalloped bottom edge, like
  little feet, exactly like the reference.
- Colour: flat warm orange `#FF8A1F` body. One soft glossy shine: a single lighter (white, about 35 %)
  rounded highlight on the upper left of the body. No gradients beyond that one shine, no outline,
  no drop shadow, no glow, no sparks, no background elements.
- Face: two white round eyes with black pupils (`#1F2328`), a small simple mouth line, like the
  reference. Friendly, a little cheeky. No eyebrows, no cheeks, no arms, no legs, no hands.

## Make these images (each its own file, square 1024 × 1024, the flame centred, filling about 70 %
of the height, on a perfectly flat, plain background colour `#D6ECFF` with nothing else on it)

1. `docs/redesign/flame/flame-happy.png` - idle, content, small smile, eyes looking at the viewer.
2. `docs/redesign/flame/flame-joy.png` - thrilled: tips flared up taller, eyes squeezed into happy
   arcs, wide open smiling mouth.
3. `docs/redesign/flame/flame-out.png` - the streak is lost: the SAME character but small, low and
   rounded like the "upset" one in the reference (no tall tips, just a soft dome with tiny bumps),
   flat cool grey `#B7C0C9` body, droopy half-closed eyes, small frown. Same white shine.
4. `docs/redesign/flame/flame-proud.png` - a long streak: confident sly smile, half-lidded eyes
   like "sly" in the reference, tips standing tall.

Then make one overview `docs/redesign/flame/flame-sheet.png` (1536 × 1024, white background) with the
four flames side by side and their names (happy, joy, out, proud) in small grey text underneath.

Keep the same character, proportions, colour and eye style in all four. Do not modify any other files.
