# Duolingo mechanics: evidence for NATKØRSEL adaptation

Research date: 2026-10-12. Sources below are Duolingo primary sources or a Duolingo-authored research paper. This is a product-mechanics review, not proof that any mechanic teaches safe driving. Duolingo’s efficacy studies concern language outcomes; transfer to Danish road rules, visual hazard detection, or real driving is unvalidated.

## Evidence table

The adaptation column contains research candidates, including ideas deliberately rejected during implementation. The final route covers 58 existing sections across 10 chapters, all open. It has no mastery lock, energy budget or automatic prose grading. See [the implemented decisions](duolingo-applied.md) and [engine contract](journey-engine.md) for the final behaviour.

## First-principles design for NATKØRSEL

The transferable core is a control loop, not Duolingo’s branding or its exact reward economy:

1. **Orient:** show one observable road cue and the single rule family being trained.
2. **Attempt:** require a decision before revealing the explanation.
3. **Explain:** identify the cue, rule, and safe action in Danish; cite the approved rule version internally.
4. **Vary:** ask a new scene with the same underlying distinction, including a near-miss distractor.
5. **Schedule:** revisit the rule after a delay, mixing it with other rule families.
6. **Measure:** track delayed mixed-set recall, transfer to unseen photos/scenes, and explanation quality—not streak length, XP, or completion alone.

With 83 questions and 58 micro-lessons, the immediate opportunity is to create item families rather than merely add more content: each authored question should have at least one altered visual/context variant, a misconception label, a short explanation, and a later transfer item. The 17 garage areas can provide the path hierarchy; the 3D game should be the application/transfer layer, not a substitute for rule retrieval.

## What cannot be copied or inferred

- Duolingo’s language outcomes cannot be treated as evidence that NATKØRSEL improves Danish theory-test scores, hazard perception, lawful decisions, or on-road behaviour. Duolingo’s own efficacy report measures beginner reading/listening and describes language-learning outcomes. [Duolingo efficacy report](https://static.duolingo.com/s3/DuolingoReport_Final.pdf) (2021).
- Retention lifts from streak or quest A/B tests support continued app use, not durable knowledge or safer driving.
- A Duolingo-style energy/hearts gate is not automatically appropriate for safety education: punishing errors can suppress the very repair practice that matters. Treat the 2025 Energy post as a limited experiment, not a settled best practice.
- XP, leaderboards, animations, characters, and a linear path are engagement interfaces. They do not prove mastery; avoid rewarding speed, guessing, or volume.
- Duolingo’s HLR model and language practice data cannot be transplanted uncalibrated. NATKØRSEL needs its own item difficulty, misconception, delay, and transfer data, with no claim of human learning evidence until measured.
- Legal content must remain versioned and human-reviewed. A generative “explain my answer” layer can draft wording, but it must not author Danish road law or resolve ambiguity without an approved source.
