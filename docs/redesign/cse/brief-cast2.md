# Codex image brief: Blink, Bobbel, Krabbe and Dæk, designed like Sneglen and Kegle

Use your image generation tool (image_gen.imagegen). The ONLY reference is the attached
`docs/redesign/cse/sneglen-og-keglen.png`: our snail and our traffic cone, drawn in the style of the animated series
Common Side Effects. The two new sheets must look like they come from that same sheet, drawn by the same artist:

- the same fine, slightly irregular dark brown-black ink outline;
- the same FLAT, muted, slightly dirty colours with one hard-edged shadow tone, and the same worn print texture:
  small scuffs, speckles, chipped edges, faded paint (look at the cone's rust spots and the snail's speckles);
- the same deadpan faces: small round eyes with heavy half-closed lids that look sideways, thin eyebrows, a tiny
  understated mouth (a smirk, a small open mouth when talking), never big shiny cartoon eyes, never a wide grin;
- the same limbs as the cone: thin brown rubbery arms with simple brown mitten hands, no gloves with highlights;
- the same proportions logic: the object stays an object, the face sits on it;
- NO glossy highlights, no gradients, no 3D render look, no Pixar or emoji look, no chibi, no shoes with soles.

Two sheets, each laid out EXACTLY like the reference: 2 rows x 3 poses, one character per row, generous white space
between them, every figure complete, on a plain WHITE background, 1536 x 1024:

1. `docs/redesign/cse/cast2/sheet-a.png`
   - Row 1, BLINK: a ladybird the size of a cat, with a faded mustard (#D9A93A) shell with hard-edged black spots and a
     dull brown head and legs; two short antennae with round tips. Poses: (1) neutral, half-lidded, legs planted;
     (2) pointing ahead with one front leg-arm, small open mouth; (3) a dry thumbs-up with a mitten hand, smirk.
   - Row 2, BOBBEL: a small worn mint-green (#8FB9A0) bubble microcar (Isetta-like) with a cream roof; its headlights
     are its half-lidded eyes and its chrome bumper is a small flat smile; four small wheels. Poses: (1) neutral,
     three-quarter view; (2) leaning back on its rear wheels, mildly surprised; (3) a small bounce, pleased smirk.
2. `docs/redesign/cse/cast2/sheet-b.png`
   - Row 1, KRABBE: a dull red crab (#B23A2E) driving a small faded mustard classic car, its eye stalks above the
     windscreen with half-lidded eyes, one claw resting out of the side window, crab legs poking out under the car.
     Poses: (1) neutral, three-quarter; (2) waving the claw, small open mouth; (3) a dry thumbs-up claw, smirk.
   - Row 2, DÆK: a worn black car tyre standing upright with dusty grey rubber and a dull cream hubcap; the face sits
     on the hubcap; thin brown arms and legs with mitten hands like the cone. Poses: (1) neutral, half-lidded;
     (2) one hand raised, talking; (3) a dry thumbs-up, smirk.
