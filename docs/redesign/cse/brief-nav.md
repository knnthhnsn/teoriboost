# Codex image brief: the TeoriBoost menu icons, drawn as one proper set

Use your image generation tool (image_gen.imagegen). Attached: `docs/redesign/cse/sneglen-og-keglen.png` and
`docs/redesign/cse/biler.png` (the STYLE: the animated series Common Side Effects: confident, slightly irregular dark
ink outlines, flat muted cel colours with one sharply edged shadow tone; palette smoky teal, dusty olive, faded
mustard #E8B530, rust-orange, warm cream, charcoal) and `docs/redesign/cse/split/sneglen-3.png` (Sneglen, our snail).

These six icons are the app's bottom menu. They are shown at 28 px on a white page, on a dark graphite page
(#1F2328) and on a signal-yellow active tab (#FFD60A), so they must read on ALL THREE. Draw each as a die-cut
STICKER: the object with a thick dark ink outline, then a clean, even, solid white sticker border (about 6 % of the
icon size) all the way round its silhouette, then nothing. No drop shadow, no glow, no background tile or circle.

Make them one consistent family: the same view (straight on or a slight three-quarter), the same outline weight, the
same sticker border, and each object FILLS the square the same way (about 86 % of the canvas, centred, compact
square-ish silhouettes; do not draw long thin objects). Big simple shapes, few details, readable at 28 px. Colour
each so it has enough light and mid tones to read on dark graphite too (never an all-charcoal object).

Each a square 512 x 512 PNG with a real TRANSPARENT alpha background (no checkerboard). Save as
`docs/redesign/cse/nav/<name>.png`:
1. `wheel` (Lær): a chunky round steering wheel seen straight on, warm cream rim with a charcoal grip, three spokes,
   a faded-mustard horn pad in the middle.
2. `light` (Øv): a short, chunky traffic light seen straight on, faded-mustard housing with small visors, red and
   amber lamps dark, the GREEN lamp clearly lit (#58CC02) with a little white highlight.
3. `exam` (Prøve): a clipboard with a cream test sheet and a big teal tick.
4. `car` (Min bil): a small round mustard hatchback seen from the FRONT three-quarter, compact and tall enough to
   fill the square (like a toy car), dark windows with a highlight.
5. `snail` (Spørg AI): Sneglen's HEAD as an icon, the whole of it and nothing else: his mustard face with the
   friendly closed-mouth smile and both eyes on their two stalks with the half-lidded look, as in the attached
   snail, composed to fill the square. No body, no caravan, nothing cut off at the edges.
6. `profile` (Din profil): a driving licence card held upright at a slight angle with a simple head-and-shoulders
   photo and two lines, teal header band, cream card.

Do not modify any other files.
