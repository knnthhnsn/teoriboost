# Codex image brief: Natkørsel car-related mascots, hero exploration

Use your image generation tool (image_gen.imagegen). Generate exactly 6 images, one per character below.
Save each PNG to `docs/redesign/characters-v4/<slug>/hero.png` in this repository. Do not modify any other
files. When done, list the saved file paths.

## Shared direction (all images)
- Mascots for NATKØRSEL, a Danish driving-theory learning app for 17–30-year-olds. The characters MAY be
  car/driving related, but must be completely original and must NOT resemble Pixar's Cars (no eyes in
  the windshield), Thomas the Tank Engine, Robocar Poli, Duolingo, Tolan or any existing character.
- Same quality and appeal as a top app mascot: "aged-up cute", warm, witty, a clear personality.
- Style: stylized 3D character render, soft toy-like materials, gentle subsurface scattering, rounded
  appealing forms, soft studio lighting. Consistent style across all 6 so they could be one cast.
- One character only, full body, centred, 3/4 view, expressive pose, strong readable silhouette.
- Plain flat cream background (#F6F3EE), soft contact shadow, no scenery, no text, no logos, no
  license plates with text, no frame.

## Characters

### bobbel: Bobbel, the little bubble car
A tiny round vintage microcar creature, egg-shaped like a 1950s bubble car, in soft mint green with cream
details. Its two round headlights are its eyes, with expressive chrome eyelids/brows; the front bumper is
a small curved smile. Stubby wheels it can wiggle like feet. Personality: eager, warm, a bit over-careful
("tjekker spejlet tre gange"). Pose: bouncing up proudly on its back wheels, headlight eyes sparkling.

### kegle: Kegle, the nervous traffic cone
A soft, squishy orange traffic cone creature made of glossy rubber, with a white reflective stripe like a
belt, rounded tip, big worried expressive eyes on the upper cone, tiny arms that grab its own stripe, short
rubbery base it waddles on. Personality: an anxious overthinker who always wobbles but never falls over.
Pose: wobbling mid-panic, leaning, arms up, sweat drop.

### sneglen: Snegl, the careful snail
A calm, wise snail whose shell is a small rounded vintage car body (cream and deep teal, with tiny round
windows, little chrome bumper and two small wheels tucked under), so the shell looks like a cosy camper car.
Soft pale-olive snail body, long eye stalks with sleepy, kind half-lidded eyes. Personality: the slow,
unbothered driving coach ("hastighed er ikke en personlighed"). Pose: leaning out of the shell, one eye
stalk raised in a knowing look.

### krabbe: Krabbe, the hermit crab in a toy car
A coral-red hermit crab who uses a small vintage toy car (mustard yellow, rounded, no text) as its shell,
peeking out of the driver's window with big expressive stalk eyes and one oversized claw resting on the roof
like a confident driver's elbow out the window. Personality: cocky but lovable, always "lige flyttet ind".
Pose: elbow (claw) out the window, grinning, the toy car tilted mid-turn.

### daek: Dæk, the chubby tyre coach
A plump, soft rubber car tyre character (charcoal with a warm brown undertone), a donut-shaped body with
visible chunky tread pattern, a shiny silver rim as a round face plate with big friendly eyes and bushy
grey eyebrows, short thick arms and legs. Personality: a calm, encouraging old coach. Pose: rolling
forward and giving a big thumbs-up, wink.

### blink: Blink, the turn-signal beetle
A small round beetle whose glossy shell is amber like a car's turn-signal lens, faceted like a lamp lens;
when it's happy its shell glows warm orange. Big curious dark eyes, short antennae with little round tips,
six tiny legs. Personality: quick, cheeky, always "blinker i god tid". Pose: mid-hop, shell lit up and
glowing, one leg pointing to the side as if signalling.
